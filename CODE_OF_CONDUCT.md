# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a harassment-free, inclusive, and respectful community.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and welcoming
- Accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers.
All complaints will be reviewed and addressed promptly.
